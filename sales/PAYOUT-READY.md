# Payout Readiness — Fiverr (Thailand)

**I cannot verify your account state.** Fiverr blocks automated access to seller
dashboards, so everything here is a checklist for you to confirm yourself. Where
a figure is included it is a structural fact, not a live number — Fiverr changes
fees regularly, so read the current figure on the page before acting.

**No money moves through me.** I cannot send, receive, hold or request funds.
Every payment happens inside Fiverr. If anyone — including me — asks you to pay
outside the platform, that is a scam.

---

## Why this matters more than it looks

A gig can be live, an order can be delivered, and the money still cannot reach
you. Fiverr holds funds in **clearing** for a period after delivery, then sends
them to your payout method. If no payout method is set up, that transfer fails
and the balance sits there.

**Set up the payout method before you deliver the first order**, not after.

## Step 1 — Identity verification

| | |
|---|---|
| Where | Seller Dashboard → Settings, or the verification prompt on the dashboard |
| What | Passport or national ID, plus a selfie in some cases |
| Constraint | **The name must match your Fiverr account name exactly** |

The most common rejection is a name mismatch. If your Fiverr account says
`Krisakorn` and your ID says `Krisakorn U.`, fix the account name first, then
resubmit.

Do not submit an ID belonging to someone else. Accounts get permanently banned,
and unpaid balances are forfeited.

---

## Step 2 — Payout method

Fiverr supports more than one route out. Availability varies by country, and
Fiverr decides which ones you are offered — you cannot force a choice.

| Route | Typical for Thailand | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **PayPal** | Widely available | Easiest to set up. Must match the ID name. Thai PayPal accounts can receive USD. |
| **Payoneer** | Available in most regions | Common for freelancers. Free to receive. |
| **Bank transfer (local)** | Availability varies | Would show as a local bank option if offered to you. |
| **Wise** | Sometimes via Payoneer | Check what your account is actually offered. |

**Open the payout settings page and read what is actually listed there.** That
list is authoritative; anything written here is a general guide only.

### The name rule

For every route, the account holder name must match your verified ID. Mismatches
cause failed payouts and support tickets. This is the single most common payout
problem for new sellers.

## Step 3 — Confirm the clearing window

After you deliver an order:

1. You submit delivery
2. The buyer has a review window to accept or request revisions
3. Funds move from **pending** to **available**
4. They land in **clearing**
5. Fiverr pays out on its schedule

Check the exact durations in your Earnings page — they change. The important
planning point: **there is a real gap between delivering and being paid.** Plan
your own costs to survive that gap, because your first order will not arrive
with money attached to it.

### Your first order has no reviews

Buyers new to Fiverr are more cautious, so early orders often take longer to
clear. This is normal and resolves once you have reviews.

---

## Step 4 — Money held by Fiverr versus your own payment provider

Two separate things, easy to confuse:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fiverr's fee** | Taken when Fiverr releases funds to your payout method |
| **Your provider's fee** | PayPal/Payoneer may charge a receiving or conversion fee |

Both come out of the order value. On a $149 order, two layers of fees plus
currency conversion can shave a meaningful percentage off what actually reaches
your account. Before quoting a client, work out the net so you do not accept a
price that loses you money.

## Step 5 — Tax position

**I am not a tax adviser and this is not tax advice.** Get a real answer from a
Thai accountant (ผู้ตรวจสอบบัญชี) once you have actual income.

Two things worth knowing now:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Form W-9** | A US IRS form for people with a US taxpayer ID. **Not applicable to you.** Fiverr shows it on a generic checklist. Skip it. Do not enter invented US details. |
| **Thai obligations** | Earning from online work may need declaring in your annual return. The thresholds and treatment depend on your situation — ask an accountant. |

## Order-to-bank timeline you can expect

| Stage | What you do | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| Order arrives | Accept, send the requirements form | Order in progress |
| Scope agreed | Write it into the order's `scope.md` | Written record exists |
| Build and test | Run the checklist | `RESULT.md` filled with real output |
| Deliver | Submit delivery on Fiverr | Status: delivered |
| Review window | Wait, respond to revisions quickly | Funds move |
| Clearing | Nothing to do | Balance pending |
| Payout | Automatic on Fiverr's schedule | Money in your account |

**The only step where you can lose a dispute is the scope step.** Once it is
written and agreed, you have evidence. This is why the intake form is
non-negotiable.

## Pre-flight check before you accept an order

- [ ] Payout method set up and verified, with a matching name
- [ ] You know the net amount after Fiverr's fee and your provider's fee
- [ ] Requirements form sent and answered **before** you start
- [ ] Scope written into the order folder, with what is excluded listed
- [ ] Delivery date in your order folder is later than Fiverr's deadline
- [ ] You know your revision count and what counts as a new feature
